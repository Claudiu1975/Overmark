Overmark watches the speed of this device with GPS.

While the speed stays over a limit you choose, a short beep marks every second. After 45 seconds an alarm sounds and the speed flashes red. Drop under the limit and the count starts over.

The limit starts at 90 km/h and moves in steps of 0.5 km/h. The app opens in the language of the device. A language you choose stays on that device.

Only the language and the speed limit are kept. Leaving the app clears temporary files. There is no account and no analytics.

If the screen is off and the speed is over the limit, Overmark posts a notification that can show on the lock screen.
