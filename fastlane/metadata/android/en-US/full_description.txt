Overmark Pro watches the speed of this device with GPS.

While the speed stays over a limit you choose, a short beep marks every second. After 45 seconds an alarm sounds and the speed flashes red. Drop under the limit and the count starts over.

A floating gauge stays on top of other apps. It comes in a small and a large size. The ring lasts 60 seconds: the first 45 are amber, the last 15 are red. Over the limit the number turns red and heavier, and at 45 seconds it flashes. Tap the gauge to open Overmark.

The limit starts at 90 km/h and moves in steps of 0.5 km/h. The app opens in the language of the device. A language you choose stays on that device.

Only the language, the speed limit, and the gauge size are kept. Leaving the app clears temporary files. There is no account and no analytics.

If the screen is off and the speed is over the limit, Overmark posts a notification that can show on the lock screen.
